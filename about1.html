About Us

Our Aims and Objectives

  • To provide supported living at it's best with the highest quality care and support.
  • To provide holistic, high quality person-centered care and support to service users.
  • To provide supportive service that considers individual service user’s choices, beliefs, wishes and abilities
  • To deliver a progressive planned pathway for each individual which promotes their independence and safety.
  • To promote equality and diversity through a service free of discrimination of any sort.
  • To continually provide a proactive and responsive service.
  • To provide personalised, innovative, effective, creative and affordable service.
  • To be a first-choice employer in our local area known for its high standard values.
  • To Maintain business financial viability and growth for long term benefit to the community.
  • To Build a committed relationship and work ethics with the multidisciplinary team (family, health and social care team) for effective provision of care for each service user.
  • To Prevent admissions and promote prevention incoporating well-being services.
  • To Provide a service that focuses on outcome: recovery and independence and long-term solutions including self management.
