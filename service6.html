Lumina Wellness, Exercise & Sports Therapy Pathway

At Lumina Living Care, we provide supported living for individuals with learning disabilities and autism, with a unique focus on holistic well-being. Our Lumina Wellness, Exercise, and Sports program sets us apart by integrating physiotherapy, tailored sports, and mindful movement to enhance physical, mental, and emotional health.

We believe that everyone deserves an active and fulfilling life, regardless of mobility challenges. Our specialized physiotherapy services support wheelchair users and those with limited mobility, while our adaptive sports and sensory-friendly activities ensure that all individuals can engage in movement at their own pace. From team games and yoga to hydrotherapy and dance, we create personalized wellness plans that empower our residents to build confidence, improve health, and enjoy social connections.

At Lumina, wellness isn’t just about fitness—it’s about independence, joy, and inclusion. We are committed to helping individuals thrive in a supportive, engaging, and active environment.

Join us and experience the Lumina difference—where movement transforms lives!
