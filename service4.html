Transforming Care

Our aim is to assist people moving from a hospital setting to successfully transition to living in their own accommodation, preventing the likelihood of a placement breakdown, leading to a subsequent re-admission to hospital.

We offer intensive care within registered environments, which are safe and homely. We also provide bespoke supported living solutions, to further support people if required, through their care pathway.

Care services for people with complex needs

Our staff teams have significant expertise in supporting people with complex needs. We focus on developing the individuals’ skills and helping them to become more independent, in preparation for moving into their own tenancy

The services that we offer include:

  • A range of accommodation including individualised flats, each with their own activities of daily living (ADL) kitchens, designed to promote independence whilst maintaining safety
  • Spacious communal areas and well-maintained gardens that are safe and private
  • Locations with access to the wider community and close to amenities.
  • Supported living services offering a care pathway from our team as required
  • Innovative person-centred support programmes and personal activity planners
  • Staff teams with significant expertise in supporting people with complex needs, including dedicated positive behaviour support (PBS) specialists
  • A range of daily activities, with an emphasis on developing life and community skills
  • Intensive support ratios with 1:1 specialist support available, as required
  • Progression through a care pathway to live as independently as possible
  • Access to education and supported employment opportunities
  • Public transport nearby to many services
