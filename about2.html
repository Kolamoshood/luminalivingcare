About Us

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Our Values

our values are what unite and guide us to deliver the best for the individuals we support. They underpin our purpose of supporting opportunity, choice and success. They define what we do and how we do it, inspiring us to achieve high standards and driving us to exceed them.

Our values inspire us to succeed, for our organisation, for our colleagues – and, above all, for the individuals we support.

Our values show our determination to be the best and to do the best for the individuals we support, their families and the commissioners we work with.

  • Quality: we believe in providing high and best quality Care and support for our service users beyond their expectations to make us stand out
  • Choice and Respect: we believe everybody should express choices freely about their lives and we will encourage this and uphold the rights of our service users and all members of the multidisciplinary team
  • Commitment to providing safe care and support through prevention, rehabilitation and recovery to achieve maximum independence. Seeing potential, seeing what could be and not limitations. Being creative and trying out new things to find the best solutions that make it happen
  • Ensuring equality and Inclusion: we believe everybody should be treated equally and fairly regardless of their disabilities so they live happily with a sense of inclusion
  • Enabling socialisation with the help of support groups: Being a loyal and true friend to the people we support, including the families of the people in our care.Our business is about people, bringing people together in a healthy space for hope and a better future.
  • Accountability for every aspect of our servicewe are always open and transparent in what we do and always try to do our best, questioning everything in a positive and constructive way and always striving to improve.

Our Mission

Lumina Living Care LTD is committed to providing high quality care and support with respect and dignity to service users to enable them have the best quality of life.

Our Vision

To be a world class service known for enhancing quality of life, empowering service users to reach their highest potentials with optimum recovery, physical well-being, skills, career acquisition and self-management in highly supportive care environment.
