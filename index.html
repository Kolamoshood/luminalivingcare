About Us

Lumina living care is a supported living service for adults, aged 18–65 years who have learning disabilities, autism, and/or mental health conditions and those who present with behaviours that may challenge and do not need to be managed as hospital in-patients. Our services are bespoke, person-centred care provided in Luton and surrounding area. We recognise the difficulties and challenges our potential clients may face finding their own homes and managing their activities of daily living, therefore we provide services that enable them have their own choice and control over their lives by providing them with their own tenancy and care agreements.

We provide our care in an environment where people can feel at home, which allows our service users to have more choice about the type of care they receive and remain closer to friends, family and loved ones.

Covid 19 Safety

Covid 19 pandemic has brought on various challenges in service delivery. We are committed to ensuring the people who live in our services are safe, as well as recieving adequate supportive care which means they continue to thrive despite the challenges faced.

We have robust procedures we have put in place. We are ensuring all risks are minimised not only for people joining our services but for individuals already living there, and all our team members through use of PPE, infection control, robust cleaning, testing and vaccination.

Our Care Philosophy

Lumina Living Care ‘Principles of Good Adult Care’ is based upon the key principles of privacy, dignity, self-fulfilment, independence, choice, safety and rights. This is integral to our working practices.

The care staff at Lumina Living Care will work towards positively developing individual qualities, which will give each Service User an opportunity for growth and development.

It is a belief of the organisation and staff working at Lumina Living Care that change is always possible. Each individual makes choices although not always with conscious awareness of themselves or of others. Any change in behaviour, thinking or feeling becomes the basis for wider changes to follow. All adults are therefore valued and are of value and are not written off or labelled because of their behaviour or mental ill health.
